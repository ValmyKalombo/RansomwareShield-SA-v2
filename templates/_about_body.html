RansomwareShield-SA

See your ransomware readiness. Prioritise what matters. Prove improvement.

Who we are

RansomwareShield-SA is a cybersecurity readiness platform designed for South African organisations, with a focus on the financial sector. It was conceived from Group 7's research into why banks and similar institutions struggle to see their overall ransomware preparedness when governance, people, technology, third parties and recovery are assessed in isolation.

Our mission

To make ransomware readiness visible, explainable and actionable - turning a structured assessment into a clear score, a prioritised risk register, owned remediation and an executive report that leadership can act on.

Our vision

An organisation that knows exactly where it is strong, where it is exposed and what to do next - and can demonstrate measurable improvement from one assessment to the next.

Secure by design

  • HTTPS with HSTS and secure, HttpOnly cookies
  • scrypt password hashing, lockout and rate limiting
  • CSRF protection and a strict Content Security Policy
  • Role-based access with a protected Super Admin
  • One-use invitations and admin-issued reset codes
  • Sessions revoked automatically on password change
  • Audit trail for every security-relevant event

What it is not

  • Not antivirus, EDR or a SIEM - it does not scan devices
  • Not a ransomware detector - exposure checks show hygiene, not infection
  • Not certification - scores are project-defined guidance
  • Not for real data - this academic prototype uses fictional information only
